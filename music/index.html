    MUSIC

    I've been playing guitar since I was 10 years old, and doing some form of recording / producing sound since I was 12 years old. Since then, I've played with and contributed to a number of different musical projects. Here are the "hits" so to speak.

    Lawrence

    I work as a producer and co-writer alongside Clyde Lawrence, Gracie Lawrence, and Jordan Cohen. I also play guitar in the touring operation (among many other touring/production related jobs). Click here to learn more about Lawrence.

    The Diner

    The Diner is a writing/producing trio consisting of Clyde Lawrence, Jordan Cohen, and myself. We often collaborate with Jon Bellion, and have worked with artists Tori Kelly, Alec Benjamin, and the Jonas Brothers. Click here to learn more about The Diner.

    Inspectre Funk

    Starting with an electronic solo EP I made and released coming out of college, Inspectre Funk is the pseudonym that I use to release any of individual musical experiments. You never know what you're gonna get! Click here to learn more about Inspectre Funk

    Pow Pow Family Band

    I've been a member of the family band since college, and while I don't get to play with them very often, I am very happy to play shows or jump in the studio with them whenever I have the opportunity. The more recent Pow Pow Family Band record, titled "The Palm and Other Parts", is currently available on Spotify and all the major streaming services. Our older record "All Right" is available on Bandcamp.
